# Eight-fighter tournaments, current v0.9.4

## Release Readiness v001 — v0.10.0, October 9, 2026

The menu now offers **Learn to fight**, three optional coached rounds using the shared combat rules and exact current male/female avatars, and **Quick Duel**, the existing private two-player flow with a shareable invitation link. Lessons are disposable, untimed, repeatable and guest-free; they preserve an owned surviving fighter and keep the condensed creator unchanged. Invitation URLs carry a public code and mode only, prefill the matching join field and never auto-join, replace an active match, or carry authentication.

Later online rounds reserve a server-owned **four-second presentation interval**, followed by the full **20-second action window**. Early commands are rejected; bots act three seconds after opening. The final round reserves presentation before the five-second winner reveal and full mercy window. Clock boundaries survive refresh/restart in durable localhost mode; clients never choose their own deadlines.

Bots have stable **Aggressive, Cautious or Patient** styles, displayed in the roster and battle. They keep legal stats and equipment and use only resolved public information. Practice cycles these styles between duels. Styles grant no hidden-choice access or combat bonuses.

Tournament spectators can **Cheer, Applaud or Throw tomato**. Short effects stay inside each viewer's current battle/spectator frame without remounting playback. Three-second server cooldowns, six-second retention and a bounded 24-event room queue apply. Public reads create no guest or seat; first sending obtains an ordinary guest only. Current duelists receive effects but cannot send, and reactions grant no move, equipment, mercy or ballot authority. Reaction history is process RAM only even in durable localhost mode; command retries do not duplicate a throw.

The ninth weapon is **Trident & Net**, supplied through additive equipment overlay v015 with the exact v014 dagger entry retained. **Entangle** trades damage for a +3 stamina surcharge on the rival's Strike/Technique next round only. Guard reduces its damage, prevents the net and clears an existing net; Recover clears it when executed. Nets never stack and expire at the end of that next round, death, forfeit or duel completion. Exact command costs include the surcharge, while base equipment values and saved identities stay intact. The authored trident and offhand net follow existing grips; source catalogs, heads, bodies, hands, helmets and every prior asset remain preserved.

Only **Where the Stars Remember** remains active as music. Active manifest public/audio/soundtrack-v002/manifest.json contains no battle playlist or recorded victory cue. Menu/arrival continuity, mute, separate volume settings and hidden-page pause remain; procedural gameplay effects remain enabled. All v001 battle tracks, the old victory clip, original soundtrack sources and previous releases are preserved for the user's later song selection. Battles, their entrance and verdict sequences have no score music.

Free temporary hosting and session-scoped records remain the selected scope. Durable online progression, coins and unlocks remain separate milestones. ## Player flow

1. A first visit in a browser tab plays the preserved 20-second sky-to-arena Flux flight. Skip intro or Escape continues immediately. Reduced motion offers an arena still, Enter, and an optional deliberate playback choice. Completion is remembered in that tab's session storage.
2. NAME YOUR FIGHTER. precedes the compact character creator. Allocate 20 attribute points, choose a trait and appearance, then press Enter tournament. Surviving saved fighters retain their fixed identity, attributes, trait, and records.
3. Enter tournament automatically joins the oldest waiting lobby or creates one. Share the six-character code, or join a specific waiting lobby with the friend-code control. Eight visible fighter slots show every joined player's complete arena character, name, status and record, with your own slot marked You. An empty slot fills with one bot after 20 seconds without a new human entrant. Successful new human entry resets the wait; polling, refreshing and resuming a seat do not. Each bot insertion starts another 20-second interval. Empty slots stay clearly open until filled, and bots are marked Bot. There is no host-start button.
4. The eighth fighter, human or bot, triggers a secure shuffle. The service records seeds 1 through 8 and starts Quarterfinal 1. The order is four quarterfinals, two semifinals, then the championship final.
5. Only the current pair prepares equipment. Click a weapon on the rack, armor on one of three displays, and one of four helmet choices at the existing arena gate. The full arena figure previews each selection. Equipment locks for that duel.
6. Once both loadouts commit, all eight members see the same existing eight-second gate film. When the entrance window ends, the active pair enters the battle screen and the other six enter the spectator view. The first combat deadline starts then, giving the first round its full initial 20 seconds. Reduced motion retains the static gate setting; a blocked video offers a Play gate animation control without changing server timing.
7. Duelists choose secretly; the other six members watch from the stands. Public equipment after commitment, health, stamina, choice readiness, and resolved attack animations are visible. Spectators cannot submit a move, loadout, or the winner's mercy choice; eligible spectators may vote only after the winner delegates to the crowd.
8. The winner's name and WINNER! appear for five seconds, followed by a full 20-second MERCY? window with Spare, Kill and Let crowd decide. Timeout defaults to Spare. Delegation starts a separate 20-second crowd ballot. Living spectators still in the lobby, including bots, can each cast one Spare/Kill vote; the current duelists cannot vote. Only a Kill majority executes, so ties and no votes spare. The loser leaves the bracket regardless of the verdict. A six-second intermission then calls the next pair, resetting health/stamina and selecting equipment again.
9. A spared loser alone sees You live to fight another day! and retains their identity and records. A killed loser sees the native execution, then Hades takes your soul.; acknowledged departure is followed by the preserved arrival film and fresh replacement naming. The final records one tournament title for an eligible surviving champion. Enter another tournament takes a surviving fighter directly into automatic lobby placement. Leaving the result instead opens the creator with that identity locked. A replacement receives a new identity and zero wins.

Spared eliminated players may continue watching or leave after their verdict resolves. The killed player's client returns through the death sequence to replacement naming. A future entrant who withdraws still owes their scheduled forfeit and verdict; the same identity cannot race into a different lobby while its fate remains unresolved. Waiting-lobby withdrawal removes the seat, allowing another guest to fill it.

Waiting players and public/member spectators can expand **Arena lobby chat**, write a message and press Enter or Send. Shift+Enter adds a line break while composing. Reading needs no guest or fighter seat; the first send obtains only an ordinary guest identity. The service assigns a tournament member's actual fighter name or a numbered Spectator label. Chat accepts plain text up to 240 Unicode code points, with at least two seconds between sends and ten sends per minute. The room retains up to sixty recent messages for one hour in RAM and loses them on restart in both hosting modes. Durable fighter records stay separate; chat does not grant a move, loadout, mercy or ballot permission.

## Timing and advancement

| Stage | Window | Automatic behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting lobby | 20 quiet seconds | Adds one bot to an empty seat; a new human entrant restarts the wait |
| Equipment | 120 seconds | Bots select gear automatically; missing human selections use sword, medium armor, no helmet |
| Gate entrance | 8 seconds | Opens the first action window after the film window |
| Combat choice | At most 20 seconds | Missing choices become Recover |
| Disconnect | 90 seconds | Scheduled active duelist forfeits; both absent produces an abandoned draw |
| Winner reveal | 5 seconds | Displays the winner before mercy opens |
| Mercy | 20 seconds after winner reveal | Spare |
| Delegated crowd ballot | Separate 20 seconds after delegation | Kill only if Kill votes exceed Spare votes; otherwise Spare |
| Intermission | 6 seconds | Calls the next scheduled match |

Both action commitments resolve immediately. Refresh, polling, and repeated commands do not reset deadlines. Subsequent action deadlines begin when the server resolves the previous round; playback and network delay may leave less than 20 seconds on a client's controls. The first deadline is specifically delayed until entrance finishes. A resumed gate film seeks to the elapsed point in the server's entrance window.

Lobby bot deadlines persist with the roster. Each insertion fills only one seat and schedules a fresh 20-second interval, including after a long pause. Older waiting lobbies without a bot deadline receive one fresh interval when this version first reads them. If the last human leaves a waiting lobby, it closes and adds no further bots. Due timer events run before incoming requests; a due bot can take the final seat before a simultaneous human join is processed.

Bots choose equipment privately when called to a match, then commit a legal move three seconds into each round using only the resolved public duel. Their pending choice stays secret under the same rules as a human choice. Bot winners spare defeated fighters when the winner reveal ends. Eligible spectator bots also submit paced Spare/Kill votes during a delegated crowd ballot. Bot votes, eligibility and timing persist with the match. Bots do not disconnect, create guest sessions, or receive human authentication tokens. The usual gate window, human choice deadlines, intermissions, record updates and sequential bracket remain in effect.

The v0.8.4 execution presentation follows an acknowledged Kill verdict from the winner or crowd. Duelists retain the battle frame and spectators retain their stands frame while seeing the same finishing blow, pixel blood burst and collapse. The resolved match stays mounted while newer snapshots queue; leaving interrupts playback safely. Spare, ordinary defeat and restored already-completed verdicts do not replay an execution. A defeated player whose death recovery remains pending resumes the personal death/arrival/replacement sequence without replaying the execution. Reduced motion uses brief static cues. Original art and character registration remain unchanged.

Shared combat uses the 24-round cap and health-proportion, then stamina-proportion comparison. An exact draw advances the fighter with the lower original seed number. That seed comes from the recorded initial shuffle and is preserved through restart; the bracket displays advancement by original seeding. Double withdrawal can still advance the schedule, but an absent or retired final winner cannot receive a champion title. If all entrants withdraw, the bracket may finish without an eligible champion and awards no tournament win.

## API and public state

The member gameplay endpoints require the existing authenticated guest bearer token. Public arena listing, observer and chat reads require no guest creation. Chat writes require an ordinary guest bearer without joining a tournament. POST bodies use `application/json`. Players cannot provide damage, results, advancement, champion credit or chat author labels.

| Method and path | Body | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/tournaments/enter` | `{ character }` | Resume the current tournament, join the oldest waiting lobby, or create one |
| `POST /api/tournaments` | `{ character }` | Explicitly create a waiting lobby; the default interface uses automatic entry |
| `POST /api/tournaments/join` | `{ code, character }` | Join a specific waiting lobby |
| `GET /api/tournaments/:code` | None | Read a member's roster, bracket, current match, and deadlines |
| `POST /api/tournaments/:code/loadout` | `{ commandId, duelId, loadout: { weapon, armor, helmet } }` | Current duelist privately commits equipment |
| `POST /api/tournaments/:code/action` | `{ commandId, duelId, round, action }` | Current duelist privately commits a legal affordable move |
| `POST /api/tournaments/:code/mercy` | `{ commandId, duelId, decision }` | Current winner chooses `spare`, `execute` or `crowd` after the reveal |
| `POST /api/tournaments/:code/vote` | `{ commandId, duelId, decision }` | Eligible living spectator casts one `spare` or `execute` vote in the crowd phase |
| `POST /api/tournaments/:code/leave` | `{ commandId, duelId }` | Withdraw or leave the stands/result safely |
| `GET /api/arena/tournaments` | None | Public list of waiting and active tournaments |
| `GET /api/arena/tournaments/:code` | None | Public observer state without joining or taking a seat |
| `GET /api/arena/tournaments/:code/chat` | None | Read this tournament's short-lived public conversation without creating a guest |
| `POST /api/arena/tournaments/:code/chat` | `{ commandId, text }` | Authenticated guest sends plain text under server-owned identity and rate limits |

The root view contains the eight-player roster, original seed, elimination/connection/withdrawal flags, seven-match bracket, current match index, champion, next-match timestamp, and a revision. `nextBotAt` supplies the waiting-lobby countdown and is null after the lobby fills or closes. A profile with `bot: true` identifies a service-controlled fighter. Root `you` identifies a roster slot. Nested `match.you` is either duel-local slot 0/1 or null for a spectator; these indices are intentionally distinct. Each pairing has its own `duelId`, rejecting commands from an earlier match. `mercyOpensAt` supplies the winner-reveal boundary. Public `crowdVote` contains the deadline, eligible count, aggregate counts, the requesting player's own vote and whether that player may vote; it does not expose other voters' selections or planned bot votes.

Only an owner receives `yourLoadout` before both commit. Spectators receive null there. Public `ready` and `pending` values are booleans, never private selections. The revealed duel appears after both loadouts commit; pending combat actions never leave the service. Nonmembers cannot read the member endpoint; the public observer endpoint supplies only its public tournament view. Public chat adds no seat, private choice or gameplay authority. Spectators, losing players, and earlier-match clients cannot acquire action or mercy authority by changing a browser index. Crowd eligibility is captured when delegation begins and enforced by the service; votes cannot be changed after commitment. Leaving during a ballot blocks further voting; an already accepted vote remains counted.

Repeated commands preserve their exact ID and body when retried. A command ID reused with different content conflicts; exact duplicates cannot resolve a round, execute a fighter, advance a match, or award a title again. The client reconciles a lost entry acknowledgement through `session.activeTournament` instead of creating a second entry. Deferred browser updates keep the latest server revision while resolved animations finish.
