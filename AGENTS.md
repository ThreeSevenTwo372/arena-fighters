New duels use **combat rules v4**. After both moves resolve, each living fighter restores `max(1, 2 + floor(Dexterity / 8) + trait adjustment)` stamina, capped at maximum before the round-limit tiebreak. Base recovery is 2 at Dexterity 0–7 and 3 at Dexterity 8; Vigorous adds 1 and Ironhide subtracts 1.
